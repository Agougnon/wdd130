Togo

Togo is a vibrant West African nation led by President Faure Gnassingbé, with its coastal capital Lomé situated on the Gulf of Guinea between Ghana, Benin, and Burkina Faso. The country is celebrated globally for its iconic football legend Emmanuel Adebayor, its world-famous music duo Toofan who introduced the Gweta dance style, and delicious street food like Ayimolou.
